ImPerial TeK. | Night Operations

An original technology-themed scene built with inline SVG.

Scene Description

Night Operations depicts a technology workstation overlooking a city after dark. Rectangles construct the skyline, building windows, desk, and computer monitor. Circles create the moon, stars, and signal markers, while an ellipse suggests reflected light beneath the workstation. A polyline forms the connected data signal on the monitor, a line supports the display, and a polygon forms its base. Grouped elements share colors and outlines to maintain consistency throughout the scene.

Reflection

The most challenging shape to position was the polyline inside the computer monitor. Unlike a rectangle or circle, a polyline requires several coordinate pairs, and every point affects the appearance of the overall design. I needed to consider the monitor's boundaries while arranging the points into a recognizable data signal. Working with the SVG viewBox helped me understand how the individual coordinates relate to the full illustration.

My initial color experiment used brighter background colors, but the resulting contrast made the foreground elements less distinctive. I preferred a darker navy background with blue, teal, and gold accents because those colors separate the skyline, monitor, and data signal more clearly.

Similar vector illustrations appear in software dashboards, technology landing pages, and application onboarding screens. This project showed me how simple SVG elements can be combined into a complete illustration without relying on a bitmap image.
